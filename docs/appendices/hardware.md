# Appendix C: Hardware

## Buying hardware from us

Getting all of the hardware on your own can be tricky because the parts you select may not fit perfectly. [That's why we sell fully-assembled A+ Trackballs](https://ploopy.co/product-category/trackball/aplus/). Here's why that's great for you:

- Everything is guaranteed to fit together perfectly
- Everything is guaranteed to work as intended
- You'll end up saving money - probably quite a bit of money - by not spending on extra parts

Your call. You'll sort it out.

## Getting your own hardware

This guide was a bit light on detail in the other sections, and that's because the hardware has to conform to many specifications. They'll all be listed here.

First, a complete list of all the hardware you'll need:

- 6x Small Screws
- 3x MR63ZZ Roller Bearings
- 2x 6700-2RS Roller Bearings
- 2x Magnet
- 4x Friction Pads
- 1x 1.75" Ball


We'll be going over all of them in detail. There will be mentions of *critical specifications*; if you're going to be sourcing your own parts, **you must get parts that conform to the critical specifications**. If you go off-menu, the parts will not fit the way that we intended when we designed the Bean. That might mean that your Bean **doesn't work**.

Let's get cracking.


### Small Screws

Here are the critical specifications:

- \#2
- 3/8" length


### MR63ZZ Roller Bearings

Here are the critical specifications:

- MR63ZZ bearing
- 3mm inner diameter
- 6mm outer diameter
- 2.5mm thickness


### 6700-2RS Roller Bearings

Here are the critical specifications:

- 6700-2RS bearing
- 10mm inner diameter
- 15mm outer diameter
- 4.0mm thickness


### Magnets

Here are the critical specifications:

- 6mm diameter
- 2mm thickness


### Friction pads

Here are the critical specifications:

- Any size
- Non-slip


### Ball

We use 1.75-inch diameter (45.00mm) balls. Any ball between 44.0mm and 46.00mm will work. Miniature snooker balls are a good aftermarket option.
